# App store listing copy

Draft copy for the first release, written from what the app actually does
(cross-checked against the code, not aspirational). Screenshots still need
a real build to capture from — everything text-based is here so listing
setup isn't blocked on that.

## App name

**AlterX Identity Transformation**

(App Store and Play Store name fields both allow 30 characters — this is
exactly 30.)

## Subtitle (iOS only, 30 characters max)

**Habits, journal, future self**

(28 characters.)

## Short description (Play Store, 80 characters)

**Rebuild your identity: habits, journaling, goals, and future-self letters.**

## Promotional text (iOS only, 170 characters, editable without a review)

**Private and on-device — no account, no server, nothing about you sent
anywhere. Pick who you're becoming and track every session you spend
actually being them.**

## Full description (both stores)

```
AlterX is a private, on-device identity-transformation app.

Most habit trackers ask what you did. AlterX asks who you're becoming,
then gives you the tools to actually close that gap:

IDENTITY
Pick the archetype of who you're building toward, and track every session
you spend actually being them.

REWIRE LIMITING BELIEFS
Name the belief, trace where it came from, and write down what replaces
it.

REPROGRAM HABITS
Swap an old trigger-response loop for a new one, then check in on
whether it actually stuck.

JOURNAL & LOG BOOK
Daily journaling, plus a log book for proof you stayed aligned — or a
correction when you didn't.

MESSAGE YOUR FUTURE SELF
Record a video or letter that unlocks on a date you choose, or once
you've shown up enough times to earn it.

GOALS WITH REAL STEPS
Break a goal into steps you can actually check off, with a target date
keeping it honest.

GROWTH TRACKING
Streaks, session history, and a calendar view of your activity — see the
pattern, not just today.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything lives on your device. No account required, no server, no
tracking. Back up to a file whenever you want a copy elsewhere.

Face ID / Touch ID app lock, daily reminders, and eight visual themes —
free.

ALTER-XTRA (one-time unlock, no subscription)
- Unlimited identities and habits (free tier has sensible limits)
- Weekly pattern reports
- Full-year activity heatmap
- Personal insights pulled from your own data
- All-time best streak tracking
- Shareable progress reports
- Every neon color theme
```

## Category

Primary: **Health & Fitness** (Lifestyle is the fallback if App Review
pushes back — the habit/mindset framing sits closer to Health & Fitness's
existing "mental wellness" apps than to general Lifestyle).

## Age rating

No user-generated content shared with others, no ads, no gambling
mechanics. Should clear the lowest tier (4+ / Everyone) on both stores —
confirm this once the actual App Store Connect / Play Console
questionnaire is in front of you, since the questionnaires occasionally
ask about things not obvious from outside (e.g. "unrestricted web access"
if a WebView is ever added later).
