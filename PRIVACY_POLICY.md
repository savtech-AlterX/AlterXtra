# Privacy Policy for AlterX

**Last updated:** July 2026

## Overview

AlterX ("the app") is a personal identity-transformation and habit-tracking app. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## Data Storage

**All of your content stays on your device.** Everything you create in AlterX — your identity/archetype, diary entries, future-self letters and videos, goals, habit-reprogramming entries, limited beliefs, log book entries, quick notes, and photos/videos — is stored locally on your device only. AlterX does not have a server or account system, and does not upload, sync, or transmit your personal content anywhere.

The only network activity the app performs is checking for app updates (bug fixes and new features) through our app platform provider (Expo). This update-check mechanism does not transmit your personal content — only technical information needed to deliver app updates (such as app version and device platform).

## Permissions the App Requests

- **Camera and Microphone** — used only when you choose to record a video message (e.g., for the Future Self video feature). Recordings are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded or shared unless you explicitly export or share them yourself.
- **Photo Library** — used only when you choose to add a photo or video to your identity albums, or to select a video from your library. AlterX only accesses files you specifically select.

None of these permissions are used for background data collection, and none of the resulting content leaves your device through the app.

## Third-Party Services

AlterX does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not sell or share your data with third parties, because we do not collect it in the first place.

## Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally:
- You can delete all app data at any time via **Settings → Reset All Data**.
- Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all associated data from your device.

## Children's Privacy

AlterX is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (as noted above, we do not collect personal data on any server in the first place).

## Future Changes

If AlterX later introduces optional subscriptions (Alter-Xtra), any payment processing will be handled directly by Apple's App Store or Google Play billing systems — AlterX does not collect or store payment card information.
