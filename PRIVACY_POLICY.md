# Privacy Policy for Regrown

**Last updated:** August 2026

## Overview

Regrown ("the app") is a social app for sharing before-and-after photos of climate and environmental good news —
cleanups, restored habitats, reforestation, and similar projects. This policy explains what data the app handles
and how.

## Data We Store

Regrown has a backend and account system (powered by Supabase). We store:

- **Account data**: your email address and password (passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text).
- **Profile data**: your username, display name, bio, and profile photo.
- **Posts**: the before/after photos you upload, captions, category, and any location text you choose to enter.
- **Activity**: likes, comments, and follows you make.

Posts, profiles, likes, and comments are **public** — visible to other users of the app — because Regrown is a
social platform built around sharing this content. Only upload photos and text you're comfortable making public.

## Permissions the App Requests

- **Photo Library / Camera** — used only when you choose to add a photo to a post or your profile. Regrown only
  accesses files you specifically select.

## Third-Party Services

Regrown's backend (database, authentication, and file storage) is provided by Supabase. We do not use
third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

## Data Deletion

- You can delete individual posts and comments you've created directly within the app.
- To delete your account and all associated data, contact the app's support so we can remove your data from
  the backend.

## Children's Privacy

Regrown is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
