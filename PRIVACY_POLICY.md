# Privacy Policy for AlterX

**Last updated:** September 2026

## Overview

AlterX ("the app") is a personal identity-transformation and habit-tracking app. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## Data Storage

**All of your content stays on your device by default.** Everything you create in AlterX — your identity/archetype, diary entries, future-self letters and videos, goals, habit-reprogramming entries, limited beliefs, log book entries, quick notes, and photos/videos — is stored locally on your device only. AlterX does not have its own server or account system, and does not upload, sync, or transmit your personal content anywhere unless you explicitly turn on Cloud Backup (see below).

The only network activity the app performs on its own is checking for app updates (bug fixes and new features) through our app platform provider (Expo). This update-check mechanism does not transmit your personal content — only technical information needed to deliver app updates (such as app version and device platform).

## Optional Cloud Backup

AlterX offers an **optional, off-by-default** backup feature under Settings → Cloud Backup. If you turn it on:

- **iCloud (iOS only):** your identity, diary, goals, habits, log book, and quick notes are written to your personal iCloud account, into a private storage area tied to AlterX that does not appear in your regular iCloud Drive/Files. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
- **Google Drive (iOS and Android):** the same data is written to a hidden, app-private folder in your own Google Drive (Google's `drive.appdata` scope), which you authorize by signing in with your Google account. AlterX only requests access to this app-private folder — it cannot see or touch any other file in your Drive. This is governed by Google's privacy policy, and AlterX's use of Google user data through this feature complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Backups sent this way contain your **text data only** (identity, journal/diary text, goals, habits, log entries, beliefs, notes). Photos and videos are not included in cloud backup and stay device-only.

Neither AlterX nor its developer ever sees or has access to this backup data — it goes directly from your device to your own iCloud or Google account using your own device-level and Google sign-in, the same way any other app's iCloud/Drive integration works. Turning Cloud Backup off, or simply never turning it on, means none of your data ever leaves your device.

## Permissions the App Requests

- **Camera and Microphone** — used only when you choose to record a video message (e.g., for the Future Self video feature). Recordings are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded or shared unless you explicitly export or share them yourself.
- **Photo Library** — used only when you choose to add a photo or video to your identity albums, or to select a video from your library. AlterX only accesses files you specifically select.

None of these permissions are used for background data collection, and none of the resulting content leaves your device through the app.

## Third-Party Services

AlterX does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not sell or share your data with third parties. The only third-party services AlterX ever talks to are the ones described above: Expo (app updates), and — only if you turn on Cloud Backup — Apple iCloud and/or Google Drive, acting purely as your personal storage under your own accounts.

## Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally:
- You can delete all app data — including your photos and videos, not just the entries that reference them — at any time via **Settings → Reset All Data**.
- Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all associated data from your device.
- Resetting the app does **not** delete a cloud backup you previously made. If you turned on Cloud Backup, delete that backup yourself from your iCloud or Google Drive account, or use **Settings → Cloud Backup** to overwrite it before resetting.

## If You Need Support

AlterX is a self-guided personal development tool, not a substitute for professional mental health support. If you're in crisis or need to talk to someone, please reach out to a local emergency service or a crisis line in your country — for example, in the US and Canada, call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline); in the UK, call **116 123** (Samaritans). If you're outside these regions, search for your local crisis line — help is available.

## Children's Privacy

AlterX is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (as noted above, we do not collect personal data on any server in the first place).

## Future Changes

If AlterX later introduces optional subscriptions (Alter-Xtra), any payment processing will be handled directly by Apple's App Store or Google Play billing systems — AlterX does not collect or store payment card information.
